About Tung Son Quang Ninh

Painting with a craftsman’s heart, keeping our word like we keep our trade

Tung Son Quang Ninh is a team of painters with many years in the trade, who have worked on countless homes across Quang Ninh — from narrow town houses to seaside homes that face sun and wind all year round.

To us, every project is more than a contract. It is a home a family saved for years to build, so the paint must look beautiful, last long and deserve the trust the owner places in us. Our painters are skilled, attentive and sincere — we work as if it were our own home.

“Customers may forget the price, but they remember the wall we painted for a long time. That is why trust and responsibility always come first.”
  • We listen and put the customer’s needs first
  • Honest advice on the right paint for your budget — no upselling
  • Proper process: no skipped coats, no watered-down paint
  • We take responsibility for the quality of our work
  • Genuine paints with labels and clear origin
  • Clear warranty — and we show up when you need us
